# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

VocabReader is primarily for self-directed language learners who read EPUB books on
macOS or Windows and want unfamiliar words, phrases, grammar, and passages to become
material they can remember and actively use. It also supports multilingual learners
who keep separate English, Japanese, Traditional Chinese, and Korean workspaces.

## Product Purpose

VocabReader connects first-pass reading, focused AI help, a contextual Learning
Library, spaced review, writing, comprehension, retelling, and speaking practice in
one desktop app. Success means the learner keeps reading for themselves, receives
help only where needed, and carries worthwhile discoveries into later recall and
active use.

## Positioning

Unlike a reader, dictionary, general chatbot, or flashcard app used in isolation,
VocabReader preserves the learner-defined reading segment and the intended meaning
of each learning item across explanation, review, writing, and speaking practice.

## Operating Context

Learners import EPUB books they have the right to use, read and annotate a selected
segment, ask AI Tutor questions in the passage context, confirm learning-item drafts,
and later practise due items or active output. The app requires a signed-in Codex
Desktop or Codex CLI installation for text AI. Optional AI speech features require a
user-provided OpenAI API key.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Early Preview desktop app for macOS and Windows.
- Supports EPUB libraries, reading progress, annotations, AI explanations, learning
  items, FSRS-based spaced review, comprehension, retelling, Sentence Practice, and
  Listen & Repeat.
- Books, learning progress, learning items, review history, and most working data are
  stored locally and can be transferred through a manual full backup and restore.
- No automatic whole-book translation, mobile app, cloud sync, or fully offline AI.
- Text AI uses the learner's local Codex sign-in; optional AI model speech and
  selection speech use the learner's separately configured OpenAI API key.

## Product Principles

- Preserve the learner's independent first reading instead of replacing it.
- Explain only the learner's actual difficulty points in their reading context.
- Turn reading discoveries into durable, traceable learning items.
- Keep immediate comprehension, spaced memory, and active output distinct but
  connected.
- Make product and data boundaries explicit rather than implying unsupported cloud or
  automation capabilities.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Product and marketing surfaces should be keyboard accessible, responsive, readable
at common zoom levels, respectful of reduced-motion preferences, and provide useful
alternative text for product imagery.
