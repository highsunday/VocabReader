Turn unfamiliar language into something you can understand

Ask AI Tutor about unfamiliar words, phrases, sentence structure, or grammar. It uses the surrounding context to help you understand how the passage works, so challenging language becomes part of a book you can keep reading.

  • Ask beside the book without repeatedly copying and pasting the source text.

Use AI to turn reading discoveries into learning cards

AI turns the words and phrases you have just understood into review-ready learning cards, preserving how they were used in the book so one moment of understanding can grow into long-term memory.

  • Skip manual copying and card setup, and spend your time understanding and reviewing.
  • Each card keeps the meaning and example that help you recall how the word was used.

Remember longer with AI examples and spaced repetition

AI creates a fresh example sentence from each learning card, so you recall the meaning in a new context every time. Spaced repetition brings it back at the right time, gradually turning new vocabulary into long-term memory.

Read one book. Grow into the next.

A reading-first loop: try it yourself, make unfamiliar language comprehensible, save what matters, then review and use it over time.

  1. 1

    Read a book worth growing into

    Import your own EPUB and follow the meaning at your pace. Mark what gets in the way without stopping for every unfamiliar word.

  2. 2

    Make the difficult parts comprehensible

    After reading, ask AI to explain the vocabulary, sentence structure, grammar, and tone in context until the passage becomes clear.

  3. 3

    Keep the words and phrases worth learning

    Let AI prepare the useful details, then save the words and phrases worth learning with their precise meaning and examples.

  4. 4

    Review, practise, and expand what you can read next

    Use spaced repetition to strengthen memory, then practise writing, retelling, and speaking until recognition becomes usable language.

Practice that grows out of what you read.
