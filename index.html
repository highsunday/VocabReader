Looking something up should not be the end of learning it.

VocabReader keeps the passage, the meaning you met, and the practice that follows connected—so a moment of confusion can become lasting knowledge.

From AI help to lasting memory, in one clear flow.

Start with AI Tutor, turn useful explanations into learning cards, review them at the right time, and keep every learning language in its own workspace.

Understand the passage in context

Ask AI Tutor about meaning, phrasing, sentence structure, or grammar in the reading segment you choose. Follow-up questions stay beside the source.

  • You decide which passage AI can read.
  • Mark several difficult parts and explain them together.

Codex, without API setup

Text AI uses your signed-in Codex installation. No separate API key is required.

Turn explanations into learning cards

Turn explanations into confirmed learning items with the intended meaning, pronunciation, collocations, examples, and their source context intact.

  • One item keeps one specific meaning.
  • Search, filter, edit, and see what is due next.

Remember with contextual review

AI writes a fresh sentence for the meaning saved on each item. Answer from context, review the feedback, then confirm the recall rating that schedules the next review.

Learn English, Japanese, Korean, and Traditional Chinese.

Each learning language has its own workspace, so books, vocabulary, and progress stay clearly separated.

From page to practice, without rebuilding the context.

The workflow is deliberately simple: your reading creates the material for everything that follows.

  1. 1

    Import and read

    Open an EPUB, choose a reading segment, and mark only what interrupts understanding.

  2. 2

    Ask and clarify

    Use AI Tutor or explain all annotations together after your first reading.

  3. 3

    Choose what to keep

    Review AI-prepared drafts before adding useful words and phrases to your Learning Library.

  4. 4

    Recall and use

    Review with FSRS, write with saved vocabulary, retell passages, or practise speaking.

Practice that grows out of what you read.

VocabReader keeps comprehension, long-term memory, writing, and speaking as distinct practices—while letting them share the same learning material.

Move from recognition to expression

Use several saved items in one passage, or listen and repeat natural chunks. Writing and speaking activity stay separate from your spaced-review schedule.

Made for readers who want more than a lookup.

VocabReader fits a deliberate, self-directed learning style—not automatic translation or passive vocabulary collection.

  • You learn from novels, nonfiction, or specialist EPUB books.
  • You want explanations tied to the exact passage and meaning.
  • You want spaced repetition to bring vocabulary back at the right time and build long-term memory.
  • You want writing and speaking practice to grow from real reading and saved vocabulary.
