Start with a book. Build language ability step by step.

A simple learning path: understand what you read, remember what matters, then use it in writing and speech.

  1. 1

    Read something you genuinely care about

    Import an EPUB and read at your own pace. Mark what gets in the way, without stopping for every unfamiliar word.

  2. 2

    Ask the AI tutor when something does not make sense

    After reading, ask AI to explain the vocabulary, sentence structure, and grammar in context until the difficult part becomes clear.

  3. 3

    Keep the words and phrases worth remembering

    Let AI prepare the important details, then save them as learning cards with the meaning and examples you will need later.

  4. 4

    Meet them again until you can use them naturally

    Strengthen memory with spaced repetition, then practise writing, retelling, and speaking until recognition becomes real language ability.
